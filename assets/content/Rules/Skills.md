A **skill** represents a character's trained ability at some activity — a weapon, a craft, a lore, a social art. Each skill is rated by a [[doc-mstrylvl#mastery-level|Mastery Level (ML)]], derived from a [[doc-mstrylvl#skill-base|Skill Base]] that the character's attributes decide; whenever the outcome of using a skill is uncertain, the character makes a test against it.

Skills are tested exactly as attributes and mystical abilities are, by the procedures in the [[doc-resolutionintro|Resolution]] chapter — a [[doc-sccsstst#success-test|Success Test]] for a single uncertain act, a [[doc-sccssvlt#success-value-test|Success Value test]] for sustained work, an [[doc-oppsdtst#opposed-test|Opposed Test]] against another character. Nothing about testing a skill is peculiar to skills. What this chapter covers is which skills exist, what each of them is for, and how they attach to a character.

# Skill Body Assignments

[[doc-character#body-part-roles|Body Roles]] specify which of the body roles each body part enables. Every skill specifies which body roles it needs. If the given role is impaired or disabled, then the associated skills become impaired or unusable. For example, Climbing might list both Manipulator and Locomotor, in which case impairment of those body parts would impair the skill as well.

When you test a skill, every body part holding one of the roles that skill lists is consulted:

- If any of those parts is **unusable** — a grievous wound, or a limb lost outright — the test **automatically Critically Fails**. No roll is made.
- Otherwise the test suffers the **worst** impairment penalty among those parts: **−5** or **−10** or worse. Penalties do not stack across parts; the single worst applies.

A skill that lists no roles is never impaired by injury, however badly hurt the character is.

See [[doc-character#body-structure|Body Structure]] for the roles in full, how parts become impaired or unusable, and how a human's parts are tagged.

# Skill Descriptions {#skill-descriptions}

## Combat

- [[skill-archery|Archery]] - Striking distant targets with bows and crossbows; reading windage and elevation.
- [[skill-bmrng|Boomerang]] - Throwing curved wooden sticks for hunting and warfare, with returning and non-returning variants.
- [[skill-dge|Dodge]] - Evading attacks by shifting body; instinctive defense in combat.
- [[skill-init|Initiative]] - Battlefield experience and tactical awareness; acting first in combat.
- [[skill-melee|Melee]] - Close combat with hand-held weapons; positioning, technique, and tactical awareness.
- [[skill-shok|Shock]] - Physiological resilience to absorb violent blows without incapacitation.
- [[skill-slng|Sling]] - Hurling stones and lead bullets from a sling with accuracy and force.
- [[skill-thro|Throwing]] - Hurling daggers, axes, javelins, and projectiles with accuracy and force.

## Combat Techniques

These are the **Folk** unarmed techniques: the roster a folk character carries. Other creatures do not use them — an animal, mythic, spirit, or construct carries bespoke strike modes derived from its own anatomy, and most of what it carries (claw, gore, beak, tail, quill) has no folk equivalent at all.

Where an entry is marked _Folk_ below, its impact dice describe a folk body part: a folk bite is `1d4` piercing where a cave bear's is `1d6+9`. The unmarked ones carry no impact dice — they are manoeuvres rather than body parts, and read Strength and reach from whoever performs them, so they hold for any creature able to perform them at all.

- [[skill-bflkbite|Folk Bite]] - Teeth, at the range where nothing else will reach; small, precise, and it draws blood.
- [[skill-bflkgrab|Grab]] - Seize a limb — to take the weapon out of it, or to hold it still.
- [[skill-bflkheadbutt|Folk Headbutt]] - The forehead driven into a face, from too close for anything else to work.
- [[skill-bflkkick|Folk Kick]] - A boot driven out — the longest reach a person has without a weapon.
- [[skill-limbblock|Limb Block]] - Warding a blow with a limb — desperate bare-armed, deliberate in a mail sleeve.
- [[skill-press|Press]] - A shove: putting an opponent where you want them, and sometimes on the ground.
- [[skill-bflkpunch|Folk Punch]] - A closed fist — the plainest thing a person can do in a fight, and the weakest.
- [[skill-trip|Trip]] - Taking the legs out from under an opponent, and the fight with them.

## Craft

Every trade below that makes an article makes it by the one routine in [[doc-crafting|Crafting]] — workshop, expense, test, result, masterwork. Each skill's own entry gives only what is particular to that trade.

- [[skill-cmcs|Ceramics]] - Shaping clay into durable pots, bowls, tiles, bricks, and sculpture.
- [[skill-draw|Drawing]] - Painting, sketching, fresco work; creating artistic and technical designs.
- [[skill-fltch|Fletching]] - Crafting bows, crossbows, arrows; producing reliable or masterwork projectile weapons.
- [[skill-glas|Glassworking]] - Shaping molten glass into bottles, goblets, vials, windows, lenses.
- [[skill-hide|Hideworking]] - Tanning furs and hides into leather goods; producing armor and equipment.
- [[skill-jewl|Jewelcraft]] - Gem-cutting, goldsmithing, creating fine ornamental metalwork and jewelry.
- [[skill-lock|Lockcraft]] - Designing locks; picking or bypassing mechanical locking mechanisms.
- [[skill-masn|Masonry]] - Quarrying, cutting, and laying stone for walls, buildings, and fortifications.
- [[skill-mtlc|Metalcraft]] - Smelting, forging, and finishing metal into tools, armor, and components.
- [[skill-mill|Milling]] - Processing grain into flour and meal using mill mechanics and stone-dressing.
- [[skill-musc|Musician]] - Playing various instruments with technical facility and musical interpretation.
- [[skill-sing|Singing]] - Vocal performance with control of pitch, tone, and emotional expression.
- [[skill-txtl|Textilecraft]] - Spinning, weaving, dyeing, and sewing fabrics into finished textile goods.
- [[skill-timb|Timbercraft]] - Forest knowledge for selecting timber and managing woodland for sustainable yield.
- [[skill-wpnc|Weaponcraft]] - Forging swords, axes, and weapons with superior balance and performance.
- [[skill-wood|Woodworking]] - Shaping timber into furniture, tools, carts, and decorative carvings.

## Languages

- [[skill-common|Common]] - The shared trade tongue that carries a traveler across realms — broad in reach, thin in nuance.
- [[skill-lang|Language]] - Proficiency in some particular tongue — the generic skill used wherever a named-language entry would overspecify.

- [[skill-cook|Cookery]] - Preparing food; transforming ingredients into nourishing, delightful meals.

- [[skill-math|Mathematics]] - Numerical calculation, geometry, and reasoning for engineering and commerce.

- [[skill-pfmy|Perfumery]] - Blending herbs, flowers, and essences into scents and aromatic preparations.
