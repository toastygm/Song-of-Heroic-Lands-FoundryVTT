A **Trauma** is harm to a character — to the physical **body**, to the **mind**, to the **spirit**, or from the forces of death (**The Pall**). Trauma is the umbrella covering every lasting harm a character carries and the ongoing processes by which they recover from — or succumb to — it.

> **Trauma vs. Affliction.** A trauma is a _condition the character is in_ — a state they carry and recover from. An [[doc-afflctnrules|affliction]] is instead an ongoing hostile _agent_ (a disease, a poison, a maladiction) that runs a course and ends in death or cure. See [[doc-afflctnrules#affliction-vs-trauma|Affliction vs. Trauma]] for the full distinction and worked examples.
