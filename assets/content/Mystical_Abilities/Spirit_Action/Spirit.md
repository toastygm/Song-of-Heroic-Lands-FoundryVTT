A Spirit Action is a discrete supernatural act carried out through an allied or bound spirit — the spirit-world counterpart of an everyday deed. Roaming the spirit world, sensing across it, and communing with what dwells in it are the typical examples; this entry is the general form from which a particular action is made.

The distinction from a **Spirit Rite** is one of preparation, not of power. A rite is a prepared ceremony, petitioning the spirit world at length; an action is done, in the moment, the way a person opens a door. Both are performed through the same channel.

**Invoking it.** A Spirit Action is a [[doc-sccsstst#success-test|Success Test]] like any other [[doc-mystabilintro|Mystical Ability]], but it is **spirit-governed**: it draws its [[doc-mstrylvl#effective-mastery-level|EML]] from the [[doc-sprtpwr|Spirit Power]] it is performed through, not from a skill and not from a rating of its own. That power is the standing conduit a spirit has conferred on the worker — an Ancestor's, a Totem's, or one of the elemental Energies' — and **without a Spirit Power to work through there is no action to perform**. The action's own modifiers stack on top of whatever mastery level the power supplies.

**What bears on the test.** The Spirit Power's mastery level first of all, so a spirit-worker improves at every action at once by deepening the relationship the power rests on; then the action's own modifiers and the circumstances of the attempt. Nothing may be performed while the worker carries [[doc-arlshck|Aural Shock]], and no Mystical Ability test may be [[doc-fatepnts#fate-exclusions|fated]].

The form is set out under [[doc-sprtactn|Spirit Action]], and the channel it works through under [[doc-sprtpwr|Spirit Power]].
