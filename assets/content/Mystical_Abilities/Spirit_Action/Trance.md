Trance is the deliberate entry into a meditative state deep enough to open the practitioner's perception onto the spirit world. Controlled breathing, focused visualisation, and long discipline loosen the bond between soul and body — far enough for heightened intuition at the shallow end, far enough to perceive from outside the body at the deep end.

It is a **gateway** more often than an end in itself: much of what a spirit-worker does is done from within a trance rather than instead of one, and the depth reached governs what the practitioner can then reach in turn. Its price is the obvious one. A body whose occupant has gone some distance out of it is quiet, unresponsive, and entirely at the mercy of whoever walks in, which is why trance-work is done in guarded places and among people the practitioner trusts.

**Invoking it.** Entering trance is a [[doc-sccsstst#success-test|Success Test]] like any other [[doc-mystabilintro|Mystical Ability]], and it is **spirit-governed**: it draws its [[doc-mstrylvl#effective-mastery-level|EML]] from the [[doc-sprtpwr|Spirit Power]] it is performed through. The [[doc-sccsstst#success-level|success level]] is read as the **depth reached**, which is what any further spirit-work performed from inside the trance then has to build on.

**What bears on the test.** Quiet, safety, and time, none of which are ordinarily available in a hurry; the practitioner's own condition, since pain, exhaustion and distraction all pull the mind back toward the body; and the Spirit Power the trance is entered through. No trance may be entered while the practitioner carries [[doc-arlshck|Aural Shock]], and no Mystical Ability test may be [[doc-fatepnts#fate-exclusions|fated]].

The form is set out under [[doc-sprtactn|Spirit Action]], and the channel it works through under [[doc-sprtpwr|Spirit Power]].
