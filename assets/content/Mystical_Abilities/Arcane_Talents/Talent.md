A Talent is an arcane knack possessed without formal training — a natural facility for one particular effect. Where an Arcane Incantation is studied, taught, and invoked by learned word and gesture, a Talent is simply _had_. It may surface unbidden in childhood, and what it demands afterwards is practice to control rather than instruction to acquire.

Because it is innate it is also **narrow**. A Talent does what it does and nothing else: a premonition of harm, a flash of another's feeling, a small telekinetic push, a knack for finding water. A character with three Talents has three unrelated knacks, not a discipline; the breadth that a taught tradition confers is exactly what a Talent lacks.

**Invoking it.** A Talent is a [[doc-sccsstst#success-test|Success Test]] like any other [[doc-mystabilintro|Mystical Ability]]. It is normally **self-rated** — it carries its own mastery level rather than drawing on a governing skill, because there is no discipline behind it to draw on. It is invoked directly, and it is **free of the Incantation Casting Penalty** that makes a powerful Arcane Incantation harder to cast the stronger it is: a Talent of any strength is invoked at its own level and no worse.

**What bears on the test.** The talent's own modifiers, and the character's circumstances as the gamemaster judges them — a Talent is not helped by tools, texts, or preparation, having none. The two standing limits apply as they do to every arcane act: nothing may be invoked while the character carries [[doc-arlshck|Aural Shock]], and no Mystical Ability test may be [[doc-fatepnts#fate-exclusions|fated]].

The rules for the form are set out under [[doc-arcntlnt|Arcane Talent]].
