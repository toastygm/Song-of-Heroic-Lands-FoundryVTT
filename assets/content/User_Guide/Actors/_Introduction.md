# Actors {#actors}

An **actor** is anything in the game world that owns things, does things, and has a sheet of its own. SoHL has four kinds, and they differ in what only that kind can do. Everything else — holding gear, carrying [[doc-actionsug|actions]], taking effects, being placed on a scene as a [[doc-tokenug|token]] — every actor can do, and is described once in [[doc-undrstndsheetug|Understanding Sheets]].

## Choosing a kind {#choosing-a-kind}

| If you are describing…                             | Use a…                         |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| One person or creature — a PC, an NPC, a beast     | [[doc-beingug\|Being]]         |
| A body of people with an identity of its own       | [[doc-cohortug\|Cohort]]       |
| A place that stays put — a keep, a shrine, a cache | [[doc-structureug\|Structure]] |
| A conveyance that carries people — a ship, a wagon | [[doc-vehicleug\|Vehicle]]     |

A **[[doc-beingug|Being]]** is the one you will spend nearly all your time in: a single person or creature, with anatomy, attributes, skills, gear, wounds, and the ability to fight. It is by far the most detailed actor in the system, and every other kind is defined partly by what it leaves out.

A **[[doc-cohortug|Cohort]]** does not replace the Beings in it. Each member keeps their own sheet; what the Cohort adds is the roster — who belongs, who leads, and how the group as a whole is faring — plus a pooled view of the gear its members have shared.

A **[[doc-structureug|Structure]]** and a **[[doc-vehicleug|Vehicle]]** are both containers with a description, and the difference between them is a fact about the fiction rather than a mechanic: a Structure does not go anywhere, so it has no occupants; a Vehicle does, so it carries a list of who is aboard and in what capacity.

## What every actor shares {#what-every-actor-shares}

- **Sheets and tabs** — the header, the tab strip, and the tabs common to all actors are described in [[doc-undrstndsheetug|Understanding Sheets]].
- **Creating one** — [[doc-crtngactitemug|Creating Actors and Items]], and for a playable character [[doc-charcreationug|Character Creation]].
- **Gear** — every actor can own gear; see [[doc-gearandequipug|Working with Gear and Equipment]].
- **Actions** — every actor can carry actions; see [[doc-actionsug|Actions]].
- **On a scene** — every actor can be placed as a token; see [[doc-scnsetuptokug|Scene Setup and Tokens]] and [[doc-tokenug|Token]].
