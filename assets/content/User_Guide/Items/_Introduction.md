# Items {#items}

Almost everything a character _is_ or _carries_ is an **item**: their attributes, their skills, their standing with a guild, the sword on their belt, the wound in their leg, the disease working through them. An [[doc-ugactors|actor]] is largely a container for items, and a sheet is largely a set of tabs onto them.

Every item type shares the same sheet layout, the same four common actions, and the same handful of dialogs. Those are described **once**, on [[doc-baseitemug|Base Item]], and every page below links back to it rather than repeating them. **Read that page first**; each type page then covers only what is peculiar to its own type.

## What a character is {#what-a-character-is}

- [[doc-attributeug|Attribute]] — an innate capacity such as Strength or Will, and the raw material every skill is built from
- [[doc-skillug|Skill]] — a trained ability, its Mastery Level, its improvement flag, and (for combat skills) its strike modes
- [[doc-affltnug|Affiliation]] — standing with a body of people, and what that standing entitles the character to
- [[doc-traumaug|Trauma]] — a wound, a fatigue, a fear, or a condition the character is carrying, and the checks that resolve it
- [[doc-afflctnug|Affliction]] — a disease, poison, or maladiction running its course, with onset, course, and treatment

Traumas and afflictions are the two halves of what harms a character; the play side of both is covered in [[doc-afflinjug|Afflictions and Injuries]].

## The supernatural {#the-supernatural}

- [[doc-mysteryug|Mystery]] — a standing supernatural condition the character _is_ subject to
- [[doc-mysticalabilityug|Mystical Ability]] — a supernatural act the character _performs_, and the test that performs it

Both appear on a Being's **Mysteries** tab; see [[doc-mystclpwug|Mystical Powers]] for how they are used in play.

## Gear {#gear}

[[doc-gearug|Gear]] is the shared parent of everything a character carries: weight, quantity, value, container, and the carried/equipped toggles all live there, and each specific kind adds its own properties on top.

- [[doc-gearug|Gear]] — the properties every carried thing has
- [[doc-weapongearug|Weapon]] — strike modes, holding a weapon, and the attack, block, and counterstrike actions
- [[doc-armorgearug|Armor]] — coverage, protection by aspect, and the worn toggle
- [[doc-projectilegearug|Projectile]] — arrows, bolts, bullets, and thrown ammunition
- [[doc-containergearug|Container]] — a bag, chest, or pack that holds other gear
- [[doc-concoctiongearug|Concoction]] — potions, elixirs, and other prepared substances
- [[doc-miscgearug|Miscellaneous Gear]] — everything else a character owns

Using gear at the table — adding it, equipping it, nesting it, moving it between characters — is covered in [[doc-gearandequipug|Working with Gear and Equipment]].
