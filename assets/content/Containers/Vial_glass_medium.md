A moderately sized narrow-necked glass vial with ground-glass or cork stopper, favored for traveling medicine kits and apothecary sets where space is limited. Small enough to fit in a pouch without taking much weight yet substantial enough to hold a useful quantity of oil, poison, or precious unguent. The sealed design prevents leaking and contamination during transport.
