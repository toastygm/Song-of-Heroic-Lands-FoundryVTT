A moderate drawstring pouch of silk, gathered at the mouth and hung at the hip, sized to carry coin and a small daily necessity. Nothing about it is sensible — the cloth parts under a knife without resistance and frays at the eyelets within the year — and the cost of one would buy a dozen leather pouches that would each outlast it. That is the whole of its purpose.
